Things I build outside of research. Some are genuinely useful, some are just for fun, and some are an excuse to be creative.

## Apps & Extensions
- [B-Town Bus](https://btb.singhdan.me): Live Bloomington Transit and IU Campus Bus tracker with saved stops, walk and leave-by times, and a native iPhone app whose Live Activity follows your bus to your stop. [Code](https://github.com/DanishjeetSingh/btown-bus)
- [Print as Booklet](https://github.com/DanishjeetSingh/print-as-booklet): Print PDFs and Substack articles as folded booklets, with page numbers and staple guides. A Chrome extension first, plus a macOS Quick Action and an iPhone app.

## Browser Toys
Small, silly things that run in your browser. Most work best on a desktop.

- [urloong](https://urloong.singhdan.me): The internet has too many URL shorteners, so this is a URL longener. Paste a link and it prints you a receipt with a comically long one that still redirects to the original.
- [Flow Lines](./flow_lines): Particle simulation using trigonometric flow fields, generating dynamic visual patterns through mathematical transformations of particle trajectories.
- [Staggered Grid](./staggered_grid): Responsive web grid with interactive tile animations. Creates dynamic visual interactions through smooth opacity transitions and animated backgrounds.
- [Matrix Rain](./matrix_rain): Generative visualization simulating dynamic symbol rainfall using procedural animation and canvas rendering. Like the Matrix movie ;)
- [Guess My Number](./guessnum): A simple game of guessing the number between 1 and 20.
- [Pig game](./piggame): A fun and interactive two-player dice game where players take turns rolling dice to accumulate points.
